# How do animals adapt to global change?

The LOEWE Research Cluster VEBITA funded through [LOEWE](https://wissenschaft.hessen.de/forschen/forschungsfoerderprogramm-loewe) uses animal sensor data to investigate how animal behavior changes along environmental gradients such as climate and land use. The goal is to develop a dynamic Knowledge Hub that brings together behavioral traits, anomalies, and indicators for different groups of species. This hub will be integrated into [Senckenberg's](https://www.senckenberg.de) infrastructure as a digital collection and made available for research, conservation, and industry. VEBITA will enable the early detection of human impacts on biodiversity and support evidence-based decision-making.
