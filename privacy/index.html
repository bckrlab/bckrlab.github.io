Privacy Policy

Datenschutzerklärung

This privacy policy explains which personal data is processed when you visit this website.

In short: this is a static website. It does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking, has no forms or comments, and serves all fonts and scripts from this website itself. The only personal data processed is the technical data needed to deliver the website, which our host GitHub processes, and whatever you send us if you contact us.

2. Hosting by GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, and GitHub B.V., Prins Bernhardplein 200, 1097 JB Amsterdam, Netherlands (“GitHub”).

When you visit this website, your browser automatically sends technical data to GitHub’s servers that is needed to deliver the pages: your IP address, the date and time of the request, the requested page or file, the referring page, and your browser and operating system (user agent). According to GitHub, the IP addresses of visitors to GitHub Pages sites are logged and stored for security purposes. We have no access to these logs.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is the secure and reliable provision of this website.

GitHub may process this data in the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). For details, see the GitHub Privacy Statement and the section on data collection by GitHub Pages.

3. No cookies, analytics, or third-party content

We do not use cookies, web analytics, tracking tools, or social media plugins. Fonts, icons, and scripts are served from this website and are not loaded from third-party servers (such as Google Fonts or content delivery networks).

4. Theme setting stored in your browser

If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is stored in your browser’s local storage so that it is applied on your next visit. This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to us or anyone else. Storing it is strictly necessary to provide the function you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

5. Contacting us

If you contact us by email, we process the data you provide (for example, your name, email address, and the content of your message) to handle your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your request relates to a contract or to steps prior to entering into one (for example, an inquiry about a position), and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, based on our legitimate interest in answering your request. We delete the data once it is no longer needed, unless statutory retention obligations apply. Emails to addresses at uni-marburg.de are processed by the email systems of Philipps-Universität Marburg.

Job applications are submitted through the university application portal linked in each job posting; that university’s privacy policy applies there.

6. Links to other websites

This website contains links to other websites, such as publishers, GitHub, Google Scholar, and university pages. Those websites are only contacted when you click a link; from then on, their privacy policies apply.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), and data portability (Art. 20). You also have the right to object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21). To exercise these rights, contact us using the details above. For the server logs kept by GitHub, you can also contact GitHub directly.
