This website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you open a page, your browser sends your IP address and technical details of the request (page, time, referring page, browser) to GitHub. GitHub logs IP addresses for security purposes and decides how long it keeps them; we have no access to these logs. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing this website securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub, Inc. participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission recognizes as providing adequate protection (Art. 45 GDPR). Email

If you email us, we use your message and contact details only to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once they are no longer needed, unless the law requires us to keep them. Emails are processed on the mail servers of Philipps-Universität Marburg.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, to correct, delete, or restrict it, or to receive it in a portable format (Art. 15–18 and 20 GDPR). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR); the authority responsible for us is the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

Right to object

Where we process your data on the basis of our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you can object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21(1) GDPR). An email to us is sufficient.
